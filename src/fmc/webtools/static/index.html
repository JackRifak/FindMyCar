Step 1 · Calibrate

Control points

Click 3+ non-collinear points on this floor’s plan and enter real-world metres. Prefer 4+ corners. Each floor has its own transform.

Step 2 · Paths

Walkable paths

Click corridor endpoints in pairs along the corridor's centre. Shared corners within ~0.3 m auto-merge. Drag either dashed edge of a band (or its round handle) to move that side on its own, so the line can sit off-centre in a wide corridor; hold Shift for 0.1 m steps. Typing a total width re-centres the band.

Step 3 · Slots

Parking slots

Click a bay on the plan, then set its ID and zone.

Step 4 · Survey

Capture locations

Click a marker to edit photos, or empty space to create a location. Each photo has its own heading (0–360° from facility north).

Refresh the site-wide search index after photo changes.
