Map layers

Choose a numbered mode above to calibrate the plan or add parking data.

Step 1 · Calibrate

Control points

Click 2 points on a marked/dimensioned feature. Enter the REAL-WORLD coordinates (in METERS) for each. Need 3+ non-collinear points, ideally 4+.

Step 2 · Map

Walkable paths

Click pairs of points along each corridor centerline (click one end, then the other). Click connected pairs so segments share corners — small gaps (<0.3m) auto-merge.

Step 3 · Mark

Parking slots

Click a slot location, then fill in its ID and zone.

Step 4 · Survey

Capture locations & photos

Click an existing marker to select it, or click empty space to create a new capture location. Each location holds multiple reference photos, each with its own heading (0-360°, clockwise from facility north).

Build or refresh the site-wide search index after adding or removing reference photos.
